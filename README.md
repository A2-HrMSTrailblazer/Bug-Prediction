The experience has been improving recently with additional "why" questions for each prompt. I also argued about some of the questionable choices from AI and asked the reasons behind them. When that happened, I prompted additional options that I found from browsing and the AI recalibrated its answers with additional information.

I have also used the AI to check the assignment with the rubrics from the Canva Dashboard. It pointed out areas that needed to be improved and suggested with its own additional information.

### 4. How would you rate your experience?
**Qualitative Assessment:** Excellent (5/5)  
It was quite a good experience working with AI on this assignment. I have also been asking it whether I could make a machine learning model as my own project for bug prediction.

### 5. What learning techniques did you gain from this?
I have learnt that additional information may usually be required to provide AI to expand its contexts.
